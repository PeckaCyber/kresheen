There are no accounts yet. Create the first one (it becomes the owner) in the pod:

  kubectl exec -it deploy/{{ include "kresheen.fullname" . }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -- kresheen create-user --email you@example.com

The password (at least 12 characters) is generated and printed once if you leave out --password.
